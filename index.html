Method: 1-minute tank mass balance. Each shift includes machine priming before treatment, the treatment draw, and post-treatment disinfection. The RO plant stops at the high-level set point and restarts at the restart level, and its nameplate rate is derated for membrane fouling, ageing and feed water temperature. Tank autonomy is the time the stored water above the low-level limit can sustain treatment if the RO plant trips.
